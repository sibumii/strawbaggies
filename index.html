Started with one stall, one Saturday.

Strawbaggies began at a local market with a folding table and a handful of bags woven over a few slow weekends. The Instagram page grew faster than the stock could keep up — this site exists so the people who find us there don't have to wait for the next market to take one home.

Every piece is still made the same way: by hand, in small runs, with natural fibre that softens and ages with use.
